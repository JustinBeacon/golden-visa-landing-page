OVERVIEW

What is the UAE Golden Visa?

The UAE Golden Visa is a long-term, self-sponsored residency permit created by the UAE government to attract and retain global investors, entrepreneurs, exceptional talents, and specialized professionals.

Valid for 10 years and renewable automatically, it offers complete independence from traditional local employer sponsorships. Golden Visa holders enjoy total freedom to live, work, study, and invest across Dubai and the Emirates, alongside premium benefits like tax-free personal income and full family sponsorship.
